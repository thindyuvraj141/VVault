VText Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

VText is a chat app. This page explains what data we handle. Contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

Data we collect

  • Account: email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication), name, username and an optional profile photo. If you change your username, the old one is retired and nobody can use it again. Your name, username, email and photo are visible to people you chat with.
  • Messages: text, photos, videos, voice messages, documents and locations you choose to send, with sender and time.
  • Activity: a green dot shows when you are online, plus typing and read status. You can turn the activity indicator off (then you also stop seeing it for others) and turn read receipts off in Settings.
  • Reports: when you report a user or message, we store the report and a short excerpt.
  • Login activity: device name, login and logout times and last-active time, shown only to you in Settings. You can turn the activity indicator off.
  • Blocks: who you block, so blocked people cannot see your username, email, photo or online status.

Device permissions

Camera, microphone and location are used only when you use the related feature (take a photo, record a voice message, share location). Notifications are optional. If you turn on the fingerprint lock, your fingerprint is checked by your phone and is never sent to us or stored by VText; VText only keeps a salted hash of your account password on your phone so it can unlock the app offline. On Android, VText can keep running in the background (a small notification is shown) so message notifications can reach you; you can turn this off in Settings > Notifications.

How data is used

Only to run the chat service: deliver messages, show your profile to people you chat with, and keep the service safe. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes it as our service provider.

Retention

Every message has an expiry time (default 24 hours, you can choose 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days). Expired messages are hidden and removed from the database. Profile data stays until you delete your account.

Your choices and account deletion

You can delete your account in the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes your profile and username. Messages you already sent may remain in other people's chats until they expire. You can also request deletion by email; see delete-account.html.

Children

VText is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.

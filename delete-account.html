Delete your VText account

In the app

Open VText > Settings > Account > Delete account, then enter your password.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete my VText account" and include your username.

What is deleted

Your login and profile (name, username, photo) are deleted. Your username is retired, so nobody can register it again. Messages you sent may remain in other people's chats until they expire (default 24 hours).
